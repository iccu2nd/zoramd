Privacy Policy

Terakhir diperbarui: 3 September 2026

1. Data yang Kami Kumpulkan

  • Account data: name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Usage data: bot activity, feature settings, and logs needed to operate the service.
  • Data teknis dasar seperti alamat IP dan informasi perangkat, untuk keamanan dan pencegahan penyalahgunaan.

2. Penggunaan Data

  • Menyediakan, mengoperasikan, dan memelihara layanan ZoraBot.
  • Sending verification/OTP codes for email verification and password reset.
  • Mendeteksi dan mencegah penyalahgunaan, penipuan, atau pelanggaran ketentuan layanan.

3. Penyimpanan & Keamanan

Passwords are stored as hashes (not reversible). OTP codes are temporary and expire automatically. We apply reasonable security measures, but no system is risk-free.

4. Berbagi Data ke Pihak Ketiga

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with third-party providers required for operations (for example email delivery), limited to what those functions need.

5. Hak Kamu

  • Accessing and updating your account data via the Account page.
  • Requesting deletion of your account and related data by contacting support.

6. Perubahan Kebijakan

Kebijakan privasi ini dapat diperbarui sewaktu-waktu. Perubahan akan berlaku sejak dipublikasikan di halaman ini.

7. Kontak

For privacy questions, please contact our support team.
